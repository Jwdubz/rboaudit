What your site is costing you — RBO Motorsports —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way someone looking for tint, a wrap or new wheels would. Your service pages, gallery and business details are well built. The leaks are the slow top of the homepage, hours that don't agree, and a few promises with no page behind them.

Checked October 16, 2026

What I found

01

The homepage is slow to show its main content.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 3.4 seconds and its largest part, the looping video at the top, at 8.0 seconds. The page is about 8.2 MB. One run.

02

Your Saturday hours disagree.

The homepage and Contact page say “Mon - Fri: 9am to 5pm, Sat: 9am to 1pm, Sunday: Closed”. The business details behind the page list Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 17:00.

03

The About page says two locations, and the site shows one.

The About page says “We own two locations in Las Vegas, NV.” Every page I saved shows one address (4343 N Rancho Dr Suite 144, 89130). A copy of the About page that came back in my search listed a second one (7035 W Sahara Ave Suite 300, (702) 541-8468), and the written answer for your name listed both. It isn't on the live page.

04

Your name changes from place to place, and the homepage title doesn't have it.

The homepage title is only “Window Tinting Service in Las Vegas, NV”. The page titles, logo and business details say “Rims By Orlando”, the text says “R.B.O. Motorsports”, the domain says “rbomotorsportslv”, and picture descriptions say “RBO Motors” and “R.B. Motorsports”.

05

Some homepage promises have no page behind them.

The homepage text mentions “car stereo installation, repainting”, and the FAQ lists suspension, tail lights, HID headlights, interior fabrication, grilles and emblem customizing. None of those has its own page (your menu has 9 service pages). The “Testimonials” heading on the homepage is followed by a paragraph about the shop and a link to your Google listing, with no customer quotes.

06

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

My accessibility check scored your homepage 97 and flagged 12 photo links carrying labels that aren't allowed on that kind of link. A separate check found 9 “Learn More” links that don't say what they lead to and 13 links with no usable web address (including the photo tiles).

What's working

Clear service pages, one for each of 9 services, each with its own title, description and heading. Business markup that includes your address, map point, phone, hours and links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Yelp. The phone (702) 654-8468 is a tap-to-call link on every page I saved, a quote form is on the homepage, FAQ, About and Contact pages, and a financing link is on the homepage. Your gallery has 102 photos, and 101 different written descriptions of them. When I searched your name, your homepage was the first result.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the quote form works, and speed on pages other than the homepage. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt blocks nothing and carries a line saying search, AI answers and AI training are all allowed (“Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes”). I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Structured data describes the business

    Your markup is typed as a LocalBusiness and includes your name, address, map point, phone (702) 654-8468, email, logo, price range, opening hours and links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Yelp.

  • Gap
    Hours in the markup differ from the page

    The markup lists Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 17:00. The Contact page lists Saturday 9am to 1pm and Sunday closed.

  • Note
    Business name differs between pages and markup

    The markup and page titles use “Rims By Orlando”. The page text and the domain use RBO / R.B.O. Motorsports.

  • Found
    llms.txt exists

    /llms.txt is published (made by your site builder) and lists your pages. Some of its text shows raw code characters, such as “&#x27;” where an apostrophe should be.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows them.
  • How your site looks and loads on a phone. I did not test a phone, so mobile is untested.

Make the Saturday hours match everywhere, add “RBO Motorsports” (and “Rims By Orlando”, if that is still a name you use) to the homepage title, correct the About page's two-location line or add the second address, and trim the weight of the video at the top.
